# Tahseel – Agentic Credit-Control Copilot

## Project Overview

Tahseel is an AI-powered credit-control copilot designed to help B2B distributors manage accounts receivable, prioritize collection activities, identify potential payment risks, and make informed collection decisions.

The system transforms receivables data into actionable recommendations for finance teams, helping them understand which customers require attention, why they are a priority, and what action should be considered next.

## Problem Statement

B2B businesses that sell on credit often manage large volumes of invoices, payment records, and customer information across spreadsheets and business systems.

Finance and collection teams may spend significant time identifying overdue invoices, prioritizing customers, tracking payment commitments, and consulting internal credit and collection policies.

Tahseel aims to streamline this process by combining receivables analysis, risk prioritization, policy retrieval, and AI-assisted recommendations within one platform.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze receivables and overdue invoices.
- Prioritize collection activities based on relevant financial indicators.
- Explain customer risk assessments using available data.
- Retrieve relevant credit and collection policies with source references.
- Generate contextual collection-message drafts.
- Support human review and approval of AI recommendations.
- Organize collection tasks and maintain audit records.
- Explore short-term cash collection forecasting.

## Core Features

- Receivables Data Import and Validation
- Risk and Collection Priority Scoring
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agentic AI with Tool Integration
- AI-Assisted Collection Message Drafting
- Human-in-the-Loop Approval
- Collection Task Management
- Audit Logs and Role-Based Access Control
- Cash Collection Forecasting

## Initial Project Scope

The initial MVP will focus on Excel/CSV uploads, receivables analysis, risk prioritization, policy retrieval, AI-generated recommendations, collection-message drafts, human approval, and task tracking.

Cash forecasting and advanced predictive models will depend on data availability and validation. Direct ERP integrations and automated external message delivery may be considered future enhancements.

## Intended Users

- Finance Managers
- Collection Officers
- Sales Managers
- Business Owners
- Authorized System Administrators

## Proposed Technology Stack

The technology stack will be finalized during implementation. Potential technologies include:

- Python for data processing and business logic
- FastAPI for backend API development
- PostgreSQL for structured data storage
- A suitable Large Language Model (LLM) for AI-assisted reasoning and message drafting
- A vector database for policy retrieval
- A suitable frontend framework for the dashboard

## Responsible AI and Security

Tahseel is intended to support, rather than replace, human financial decision-making. AI recommendations should be explainable, grounded in available data and relevant policies, and subject to human review where appropriate.

The project will consider access control, tenant data isolation, auditability, and protection of sensitive financial information.
